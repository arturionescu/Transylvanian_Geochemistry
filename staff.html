The research group members

# Different backgrounds. Different tools. One shared curiosity. Our team brings together geochemists, geologists, microbiologists, engineers, environmental scientists, explorers and storytellers, each looking at the Earth from a slightly different angle. From chasing gases and sampling waters to studying rocks, microbes, faults and isotopes, building instruments or keeping the team safe in the field, everyone brings a different piece to the puzzle. Together, we follow the signals travelling from the deep Earth to the surface and try to understand the connections between fluids, rocks and life. # Meet the people and discover what each of us is curious about.
